# The owner's direction for this style

Everything the owner has asked for, in their terms, with where each rule lives. When a rule here and anything else disagree, this page wins. Updated after round 1 v3 (26 October 2026).

## Purpose
- Short vertical explainers for Instagram Reels (and TikTok, YouTube Shorts) in a 3D cartoon style the owner admires. The style must be reusable: **the topic changes every time**; the method does not. (skill 1)
- Name of the style: **3D Cartoon Animation**, described in one card in `preview/style-card.json`.

## Story
- A script with storytelling that **keeps the viewer hooked**, not a list of facts. Hook first, early payoff, stakes, escalation, re-hook, twist, loop. (skill 2)
- **A topic question at the top for the first couple of seconds**, as a visual title hook ("How was gravity found?"). The algorithm favours it. (skill 11)

## Look
- **Skin-tone faces** (not white faces), kept for the whole series. (skills 5, 6)
- **Brand colours** in clothes and environments: Rouge Noir, Blue Coral, Cognac, White Sand, Anthracite. (skill 5)
- **Real things look real:** "the Earth doesn't look like Earth" → blue oceans, green land, clouds. (skill 5)
- **No visual glitches.** Watch every bit of the video before delivering it. (skill 13)

## Text
- Font combination: **Helvetica + Editorial New**, brand colours for highlights. (skill 11)
- Caption style: **editorial lockups** like the owner's books reel ("THEY ALL read books"): big white capitals with red italic serif words tucked under them. Not TikTok-style black-and-white captions, not pills. (skill 11)

## Sound
- If no song is given, the system makes its own music, sound effects and beat matching. (skill 12)
- **Slight sound effects on every action**, everything; use what earshot teaches about effects. (skill 12)
- A licensed track may be supplied later; the owner holds the licence. (skill 12)

## Voice
- ElevenLabs, with the owner's own account. The key is given when needed and never stored. (skill 3)

## Process
- References come as files the owner saves; never download from Instagram or YouTube.
- Study the reference deeply, build the skill, recreate, go back and forth, then save the approved result and the updated skill. (skill 14)
- Explain things in plain language.
