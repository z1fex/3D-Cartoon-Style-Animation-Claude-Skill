# Script: "Everything you know about this apple is wrong" (final, v2/v3)

**Title hook (top of frame, 0–2.4 s):** How was *gravity* found?

## Narration (107 words, 30.2 s after tightening and atempo 1.03)

| Beat | Line |
|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Everything you know about this apple is wrong. |
| 2. Early payoff | It probably never hit Newton's head. |
| 3. Stakes | Sixteen sixty-six: plague shuts his university, so Isaac, twenty-three, is stuck on the family farm. An apple drops beside him. |
| 4. The question | Why straight down? Why never sideways? |
| 5. Escalate | The Earth pulls it, he says, and the same pull reaches the Moon, two hundred and forty thousand miles away. |
| 6. Re-hook and twist | For two hundred and fifty years, that was gravity. Then Einstein rewrote it. There is no pull. Mass bends space and time, like a bowling ball on a trampoline, and the apple just rolls into the dent. |
| 7. Loop | So yes: everything you know about this apple is wrong. |
