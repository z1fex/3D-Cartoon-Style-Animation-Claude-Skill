Everything you know about this apple is wrong. It probably never hit Newton's head. Sixteen sixty-six: plague shuts his university, so Isaac, twenty-three, is stuck on the family farm. An apple drops beside him. Why straight down? Why never sideways? The Earth pulls it, he says, and the same pull reaches the Moon, two hundred and forty thousand miles away. For two hundred and fifty years, that was gravity. Then Einstein rewrote it. There is no pull. Mass bends space and time, like a bowling ball on a trampoline, and the apple just rolls into the dent. So yes: everything you know about this apple is wrong.
