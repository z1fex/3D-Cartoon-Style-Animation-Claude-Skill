# What "done" means for a film in this style

A film is accepted when the owner approves it. Before that, it must meet the bar the approved example set (round 1 v3, "How gravity was found"):

| Area | The bar |
|---|---|
| Hook | a claim in the first 2 s, plus the title question on screen from frame 0 |
| Pace | a new shot every 1.0–2.4 s; about 20 shots in 30 s |
| Retention | all seven beats present, including a re-hook about two-thirds in |
| Facts | 3–6 numbers, each landing as an object; all claims sourced |
| Acting | holds dead still; gestures and face swaps lead their words; one gag per abstract idea |
| Picture | zero unintended framing flags; zero unintended jumps; zero catalogue glitches by eye |
| Text | editorial lockups in the brand fonts (or declared stand-ins); readable on every background |
| Sound | about 80 effects in 30 s (a sound on every action), about 10 accents; measured levels on target; −14 LUFS |
| Brand | skin-tone faces; the five brand colours; real things look real |
| Honesty | the owner is told what is still weaker than the reference, and which fonts were used |
